Jinwoo Jung

Hello! I am Jinwoo Jung, an M.S. student at Hanyang University, advised by Prof. Joon-Hyuk Chang in the ASML Lab, where I work on diffusion models — controllable generation and efficient inference.

I built an audio editing model that applies an edit only to the time span specified in the instruction and leaves the rest of the recording unchanged (INTERSPEECH 2026, demo), and worked on adapting the rank of each layer per timestep and per block to reduce inference cost without retraining, on both image and audio diffusion models (NeurIPS 2026).

I received a B.S. in Computer Science from Konkuk University, and spent a semester at ZHAW in Switzerland as an exchange student.

Selected Papers

SARA: Step-Adaptive Rank Adjustment for Diffusion Inference Acceleration

Dong Hoon Lim*, Jinwoo Jung*, Won-Gook Choi, Joon-Hyuk Chang

NeurIPS 2026  ·  Main Track  ·  *equal contribution

SARA is a training-free framework that adapts the SVD rank of pretrained linear layers per step and per block at inference, accelerating diffusion at matched quality. It composes with solver- and cache-based acceleration for compounded speedups, introducing rank as a new axis of diffusion inference acceleration.

Edit the Moment, Keep the Rest: Time-Localized Audio Editing via Instruction

Jinwoo Jung, Gihun Son, Won-Gook Choi, Joon-Hyuk Chang

INTERSPEECH 2026

EMKR is an instruction-driven editing framework for polyphonic audio, built on Stable Audio Open. It edits a single occurrence of a sound within the time interval the instruction specifies, at 100 ms precision, and leaves the rest of the clip unchanged.

Branch-wise Complementary Attention for Acoustic Scene Classification

Seung-Gyu Han*, Jinwoo Jung*, Pil Moo Byun, Won-Gook Choi, Joon-Hyuk Chang

INTERSPEECH 2026  ·  *equal contribution

BCA is an attention module for multi-branch acoustic scene classifiers. It allocates channel, time, frequency, and joint time-frequency attention across branches by their receptive field characteristics, giving each branch the axis its own kernel does not cover, and outperforms existing attention modules on the TAU Urban Acoustic Scenes benchmarks.
